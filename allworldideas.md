## Spencers145's Ideas
Spencers145 was a big fan of the original Shark Game in his childhood - he grew up to code the New Frontiers mod that these ideas are being compiled for.

<ins>**The Stone Ocean:**</ins>  a world with no natural sand nor undersea currents - just hard, solid slate and stone as far as the eye can see. And your aim is to break it all down to sand.<br>
<ins>**The Scrapyard Ocean:**</ins> an ocean where heaps of metal parts litter the world, and the machines take center stage. Scavenging takes place of hunting, and the frenzy is a crowd of your own creation, composed of scraps and parts. A food scarcity mechanic was proposed.<br>
<ins>**The Ethereal Ocean (scrapped):**</ins> an ocean of surrealness in that thought escapes into reality. I haven't gathered much about it, but it seems to be slightly similar to Brian's Forgotten Ocean.<br>
<ins>**The Arcane Ocean:**</ins> a scienceless world of arcana. You take rituals in place of experimentation.

## Merged Ideas
Separate similar deas I sort of fused together into one - the conceptor of each part is credited by their Discord username.

<ins>**The Primal Ocean:**</ins> a prehistoric ocean with megalodons (as first proposed by @umbraanimo) and Helicoprion (proposed by @herrodere), perhaps that could be swapped out for trilobites (I think as proposed by @atheistcanuck) - maybe all three core frenzy members could be swapped out to make room for these three. Perhaps Meg = Shark, Heli = Ray, and Trilo = Crab. There's an idea by spencers145 (@a_guy_and_he_does_stuff) for an ocean in which you dig in underground caves with the chimaeras to the centre of the earth - but he approved of the idea to merge it with an ancient ocean... <br>
<ins>**The Ancient Ocean:**</ins> I had to split the 'prehistoric ocean' in two because there's so many ideas for it. The thing that separates Ancient from Primal is its subterranean setting - an idea by both spencers145 (as mentioned before) and @happerry. @happerry also proposed eurypterid sea scorpions, livyatan whales, nautiluses (nautiluses? nautilii? nautilodes?), and frilled sharks. The *timeline difference* between these species, though, is kinda hard to explain... However, after reading this compilation, Happerry stated that "[my] first suggestion would be that, as you dig down deeper into the world, you're also digging down deeper into time/history/the past". I also merged the Dungeon idea by spencers145 in here, so add goblin sharks to the frenzy to find treasure.

## Reworks
Reiterated versions of prior ocean ideas.

<ins>**Three Eels' Arcane Ocean Rework:**</ins> *"I've been thinking about [Arcane] for a bit now, and I've thought up some conceptual parts of it. Arcana is the main resource, and as base said when concerning arcane, there are no scientists. The lab, while still retaining the name of laboratory, instead has wizard/shaman sharks or something of the sort. This could be a potential ocean to include knowledge in, as a substitute for science as knowledge is simply knowledge, and not science. Later in the game, you encounter these huge, whale-size serpentine creatures called, well... serpents. Sea serpents, maybe. It's never stated, but these serpents are actually the descendants of the chimerae (chimerae? chimeras?), who after you left shrouded, pretty much gorged themselves on arcana (which is stated) and became the beasts you see now. One aim in the world is to find something more powerful than arcana - this turns out to be an arcana-essence fusion. Sharkonium isn't present, but you know what is? Arkite! It's arcana and sand, and it looks like glowing magenta dry ice. It can be used to make golems - namely sand golems, crystal golems, residue golems, and arkite golems! Residue golems may be replaced by another golem type, though. New frenzy jobs are namely wizard/shaman sharks, geomancer rays, and arcane hunter crabs!"*<br>
<ins>**Three Eels' Ethereal Ocean Rework:**</ins> *"Doesn't the word ethereal remind you of something...? Yep. that's right! Nothing other than the raw, etheric essence itself. I feel like an essence-y focus would seem to fit ethereal pretty well (although I might be wrong), considering that, iirc, ethereal was a greenish sea, and 'dreams escap[ing] into reality' could work with essence, when you take into account that, in the gateway, the aspects are essentially your wishes and will focused into form. Ethereal could be an essence-focused world, not too similar to glowkate's chaotic (forgive me if i'm running away with this a little, glowkate), but definitely calmer and more placid. an essence-focused world where your own desires take shape. Curiosity for sharks, for example, and loyalty and attentiveness for rays and crabs respectively. Pride for dolphins, and bravery for billfish. Etc., etc.
So, instead of 'recruit shark', it would be 'focus curiosity'."* (Note from Three Eels - Glowkate mentioned an idea she wanted to use for Chaotic, which was a recyler for frenzy members.)
